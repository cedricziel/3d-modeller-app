# CAD 11: Motion Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Joints report the motion they leave free, can be driven to a value within their limits, and instances report the degrees of freedom they have left. Agents drive joints with `move_joint`; the app drives the selected joint with a slider and animates it through its range. `cadbench` grades joint values and freedoms.

**Architecture:** Driving is pure `CADModel`: a driven joint is sent to the solver as a stricter joint whose side-a marker is moved by the value. A revolute at θ becomes a fixed joint with marker A turned θ about its z; a slider at d becomes a fixed joint with marker A moved d along its z; a cylindrical at d becomes a revolute with marker A moved d along its z. The solver, the C shim and the retry need no change. Degrees of freedom come from a rank analysis of the joints' constraint Jacobian at the solved pose (`Mobility`), also pure `CADModel`. Joint and instance results carry the measured value, the limits and the remaining freedoms. The tools add `move_joint`, `limits` and `value` on `add_joint`/`edit_joint`, and show motion in the listing and write reports. The app previews drives off the main actor (latest wins) and commits one undo step.

**Tech Stack:** Swift 6 (tools 6.1), Swift Testing, `simd`, SwiftUI. No C++ changes.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-cad-roadmap-design.md`: "Assemblies and motion" (DOF, limits, `move_joint`, drag and animate), "Agent tools" (`move_joint`), PR-stack row 11. Previous layer: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-26-cad-10-assembly-solver.md`.

## Rulings

- **Ruling: drives are composed into side a's marker, not added as solver constraints.** Revolute at θ → fixed with `markerA ∘ Rz(θ)`; slider at d → fixed with `markerA ∘ T(0, 0, d)`; cylindrical at d → revolute with `markerA ∘ T(0, 0, d)`. The shim keeps building a fresh assembly per solve, and the coincident-start retry makes the driven pose exact and independent of the guess. OndselSolver's `runDragStep` on a kept assembly is not used. Cost if wrong: a later need for velocity-level motion (dynamics) must add real driving constraints to the shim.
- **Ruling: the drivable motion per kind.** Revolute: angle (degrees). Slider: travel (mm). Cylindrical: travel (mm); its turn stays free. Fixed, ball and planar have no single value. The angle is measured from a's x axis to b's x axis about a's z; the travel is `(o_b − o_a) · z_a`. With no value set, the joint is free and its result reports the measured value. Cost if wrong: a cylindrical that must be driven by angle needs a second joint (a planar plus a revolute).
- **Ruling: limits and values that are out of place fail the joint, loudly, and the joint is solved undriven.** A value or limits on a fixed, ball or planar joint; a value or limit that does not evaluate; min above max; a value outside the limits. The tools refuse all of these, so only hand-edited files or parameter changes reach the rebuild. Cost if wrong: after a parameter change narrows a limit, the joint fails until the value is moved; the message names both.
- **Ruling: limits bound drives; free joints are not held inside their limits.** OndselSolver has no inequality constraints. A free joint's measured value is reported; the listing marks it "outside limits" when it is. Cost if wrong: a free hinge may rest outside its range until driven.
- **Ruling: the measured angle is reported in (−180°, 180°], or in [min, min + 360°) when a minimum exists; a driven joint that holds reports its document value.** Cost if wrong: none.
- **Ruling: freedoms come from the rank of the linearised constraints at the solved pose, not from OndselSolver's redundancy flags or a Grübler count.** Each moving body has a twist (ω, v); each joint contributes the rows its kind constrains (relative rotation about a's x, y, z; relative velocity of b's origin along a's x, y, z). An instance's freedoms are the rank of the null space restricted to its six columns. This is exact for redundant loops (a parallelogram four-bar reads 1, where Grübler gives −2), and PR 10 showed the redundancy flags depend on the start. At a singular pose (a toggle position) it reports the instantaneous freedoms, which can be one more. Cost if wrong: an unusual singular pose overstates the freedoms by one.
- **Ruling: freedoms reported.** A joint's own freedoms are its kind's (fixed 0, revolute 1, slider 1, cylindrical 2, ball 3, planar 3), less one when driven. An instance's are computed only when the assembly has joints: grounded 0; in the solve, from `Mobility`; otherwise 6. Failed instances report none. Cost if wrong: none.
- **Ruling: `move_joint(joint, value)` refuses out-of-range values instead of clamping.** The message gives the range; `free: true` removes the drive. One undo step, "Move <joint>". Cost if wrong: an agent retries once with a value in range.
- **Ruling: changing a joint's kind drops a value and limits that do not fit the new kind** (a different motion, or none), and the summary says so. Cost if wrong: none; undo restores them.
- **Ruling: the app drives joints with an inspector slider and an Animate button, not a viewport drag.** The viewport has no instance picking or axis-projected dragging; building them is more than this layer needs, and the slider drives every drivable joint exactly within its limits. Cost if wrong: users cannot grab a part in 3D to move it; a later layer can add it on top of the same preview.
- **Ruling: previews never edit the document.** While the slider moves or an animation runs, the app rebuilds the document with the new value off the main actor, one rebuild in flight and only the newest value queued, and shows that result. Releasing the slider commits one undo step. An animation sweeps min → max at a fixed rate (60°/s or 40 mm/s) and stops at the end, leaving the end pose as a preview; "Keep" commits it as one undo step and any document change or reselection drops it. A full rebuild per preview frame is used, without caching the part stage. Cost if wrong: a slow preview on heavy parts; a part-stage cache can be added later.
- **Ruling: the slider range.** The limits when both exist; a revolute without both limits uses −180°…180° and animates 0°…360°; a travel joint without both limits has no slider or animation, only a value field. Cost if wrong: none.
- **Ruling: bench checks.** `jointValue { joint, value, tolerance = 0.01 }` compares a joint's reported value; `instanceFreedoms { instance, equals }` compares an instance's reported freedoms. `unchangedExcept` compares joint values. Cost if wrong: none.

## Review Focus

1. **A value outside the limits in a hand-edited file, or limits narrowed by a parameter change.** Expected: that joint fails naming the value and the range, is solved undriven, nothing else fails. Pinned in Task 1 (`valueOutsideLimitsFails`, `limitsFromParameters`).
2. **A redundant closed loop** (a parallelogram four-bar, a revolute plus a cylindrical on one axis). Expected: freedoms 1 per moving link, not a negative or zero count. Pinned in Task 2 (`fourBar`, `redundantPair`).
3. **Driving a joint whose instance starts far away or rotated.** Expected: the driven pose is exact regardless of the guess. Pinned in Task 3 (`hingeAt90FromRotatedStart`).
4. **Changing a joint's kind after it was driven.** Expected: the value and limits in the wrong unit are dropped and reported, never reinterpreted. Pinned in Task 4 (`kindChangeDropsDrive`).
5. **Fast slider moves in the app.** Expected: at most one rebuild in flight, the newest value always shown last, no document edit until release. Pinned in Task 6 (`latestWins`).

### Task 1: Drives, limits and measured values in the model

**Produces:**

```swift
public enum JointMotion: Sendable, Hashable { case angle, travel; public var unit: String }  // "°", " mm"
extension JointKind {
    public var freedoms: Int          // fixed 0, revolute 1, slider 1, cylindrical 2, ball 3, planar 3
    public var motion: JointMotion?   // revolute .angle; slider, cylindrical .travel; else nil
}
// Joint gains: public var value: Scalar?   (init parameter `value: Scalar? = nil`, decodeIfPresent)
public struct JointDrive: Sendable, Equatable {
    public let motion: JointMotion?
    public let value: Double?; public let minimum: Double?; public let maximum: Double?
    public let problem: String?
    public static func evaluate(_ joint: Joint, parameters: ParameterTable) -> JointDrive
    public var isDriven: Bool { problem == nil && value != nil }
    func driven(_ kind: JointKind, markerA: RigidTransform) -> (JointKind, RigidTransform)
    func measure(_ kind: JointKind, a: RigidTransform, b: RigidTransform) -> Double?  // world frames
}
// JointResult gains (init defaults nil/false/0): value: Double?, minimum: Double?, maximum: Double?, driven: Bool,
// freedoms: Int
```

`JointDrive.driven`:

```swift
func driven(_ kind: JointKind, markerA: RigidTransform) -> (JointKind, RigidTransform) {
    guard isDriven, let value else { return (kind, markerA) }
    switch kind {
    case .revolute:
        let turn = simd_double3x3(simd_quatd(angle: value * .pi / 180, axis: SIMD3(0, 0, 1)))
        return (.fixed, markerA.composed(with: RigidTransform(rotation: turn, translation: .zero)))
    case .slider: return (.fixed, markerA.composed(with: .along(value)))
    case .cylindrical: return (.revolute, markerA.composed(with: .along(value)))
    case .fixed, .ball, .planar: return (kind, markerA)
    }
}
```

`measure`: angle = `atan2(dot(x_b, y_a), dot(x_b, x_a))` in degrees, shifted into [min, min + 360) when `minimum` exists; travel = `dot(o_b − o_a, z_a)`.

Problems, in order: `"a \(kind) joint has no single motion to drive; remove its value"` (and "…limits"); `"value: \(error)"`, `"limits.min: …"`; `"limits min \(a)° is above max \(b)°"`; `"value \(v)° is outside its limits \(min)°…\(max)°"`.

In `solveJoints`, each resolved joint's drive is evaluated; the solver receives `drive.driven(kind, markerA:)`. After the solve, each joint's status is `.failed(problem)` when the drive has a problem, else the solver's. Its value is the document value when driven and holding, else the measured one from the solved placements with the unmodified markers.

### Task 2: Degrees of freedom

**Produces:**

```swift
struct MobilityJoint { var kind: JointKind; var bodyA: Int; var bodyB: Int; var a: RigidTransform; var b: RigidTransform }
enum Mobility {
    /// Each body's remaining freedoms (0…6); grounded bodies are 0.
    static func freedoms(bodies: Int, grounded: Set<Int>, joints: [MobilityJoint]) -> [Int]
}
// InstanceResult gains: public let freedoms: Int?   (init default nil)
```

Rows per joint, with ω_rel = ω_b − ω_a and u = (v_b + ω_b × o_b) − (v_a + ω_a × o_b):

| kind        | rotation rows (ω_rel ·) | translation rows (u ·) |
| ----------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------- |
| fixed       | x, y, z                 | x, y, z                |
| revolute    | x, y                    | x, y, z                |
| slider      | x, y, z                 | x, y                   |
| cylindrical | x, y                    | x, y                   |
| ball        | —                       | x, y, z                |
| planar      | x, y                    | z                      |

Axes are a's world axes. ω columns are scaled by a length L = max(1, largest |origin|) so all entries are O(1). Rank by Gaussian elimination with partial pivoting at 1e-9 × the largest entry; the null space basis comes from the reduced echelon form; each body's freedoms are the rank (absolute tolerance 1e-8) of its six rows of the basis.
